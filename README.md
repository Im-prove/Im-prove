## About

I’m a Computer Science student building across the stack—from low-level hardware and embedded systems to intelligent software, AI workflows, and practical applications.

My interests are broad but connected: I enjoy understanding how **AI, language systems, robotics, embedded hardware, and software engineering** fit together in real-world products. I learn by building, iterating, and improving systems that solve actual problems rather than just demonstrating concepts in isolation.

### Current focus

- AI / ML, deep learning, NLP, and vision-driven systems
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), LLM workflows, and knowledge-based AI
- Robotics, autonomous systems, control, and hardware-software integration
- Embedded systems, microcontrollers, Raspberry Pi, Arduino, and IoT devices
- Python, backend services, web applications, automation, and product development
